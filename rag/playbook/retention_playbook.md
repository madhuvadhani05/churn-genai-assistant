# Retention Playbook (sample)

## Month-to-month contract customers
Offer a 12-month contract with a modest loyalty discount. Highlight price lock and free plan upgrade after 6 months. Do not promise discounts beyond what the plan allows.

## Short tenure (first 12 months)
Run a 30-day onboarding check-in call. Send a welcome guide and confirm the service is working. Early support reduces first-year churn.

## Fiber optic customers
Proactively check speed and outage history. Offer a free technician visit if complaints exist. Bundle tech support for the first 3 months.

## Electronic check payers
Encourage autopay with a small one-time bill credit. Autopay lowers missed payments and billing friction.

## No tech support
Offer a free 3-month trial of tech support. Customers with support have lower churn.

## High monthly charges
Review the plan for a cheaper equivalent. A right-sized plan builds trust.

## General rules
Always be honest about offers. Never invent prices. Escalate very high risk customers (above 70%) to a retention specialist.
